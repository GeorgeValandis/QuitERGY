## Formal Keyword Criteria

- Popularity should be high enough to matter, ideally above 20 for primary targets or lower when the app can rank quickly.
- Difficulty should be realistic for the app stage, ideally 50 or below.
- The phrase must match the app's core action and user intent.
- Exact-match phrase should appear in title or subtitle when it is the main bet.
- Keyword field should contain non-repeated companion terms, comma-separated, without spaces.
- Prefer several winnable mid-tail keywords over one broad keyword with weak ranking odds.
- Validate by observed SERP relevance, not keyword volume alone.
